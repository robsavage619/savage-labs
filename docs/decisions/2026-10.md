## 2026-10-03: The volume ramp read the wrong week, and yellow was the scale's centre

**Context.** A full read of the decision spine against the live database, asking one question: is progressive overload actually running? It was not, for two independent reasons, and three smaller defects sat beside them. (1) `weekly_prescription` fed the set-progression tree THIS week's sets-so-far as its `current`, so every target was `max(MEV, done so far + step)`: it reset each Monday, moved as the week was trained, and a "hold" meant "hold at zero" early in the week. The log shows three grow muscles at an identical target for all seven accumulation weeks of the August block. (2) The readiness score's HRV and RHR subscores read 50 at personal baseline, so a baseline day with a perfect sleep score totals 66.7, a third of a point under the green line. Yellow capped intensity MODERATE (RPE 8): 32 of 39 stored plans were capped, no set was prescribed at RPE 9 or above, and the mesocycle RIR ramp and the HIGH-day-only PR re-anchor almost never actuated. (3) MEV was lifted to the curated brief's weekly range, putting five grow muscles at 12 sets against roughly 39 to 50 delivered. (4) The split's weekdays were Tue to Fri while the logged week was mostly Wed, Thu, Sat, Sun, so the reflow reported no session left on 11 of 26 training days. (5) 258 of 287 logged sets carried exactly the prescribed RPE.

**Decision.** The tree's baseline is the last COMPLETED week (`MusclePrescription.baseline_sets`); `current_sets` keeps meaning done-this-week, which is what the reflow and the direct-work floor subtract. A yellow tier caps only on a real negative deviation: HRV at or below -1.0 sigma, a score under 50, or nothing objective behind the score. The brief-to-MEV lift is removed, so a block starts at MEV and the ramp climbs into the brief's range. The split's weekdays come from the last eight weeks of sessions, and today always counts as a session in the reflow. Echoed RPE is surfaced as a data gap and nothing else.

**Why.** The vault's rule is week over week: start at MEV, add about two sets per muscle per week toward MRV, with RIR descending to about 1 to 0 by the last accumulation week (`volume-landmarks-mev-mav-mrv.md`, `overload-principle-hypertrophy.md`). Neither axis could move. The readiness change loosens a gate, which invariant 6 treats with suspicion in the tightening direction only; here the cap was firing on the scale's own midpoint, which is not evidence of anything, and the vault's HRV-guided evidence is endurance-based with modest effects. Echoed RPE is deliberately NOT nulled: dropping the RIR credit would lower every ceiling by about 5%, a penalty for a logging habit rather than a correction.

**Consequences.** Considered and rejected: recentring the subscores so baseline reads green, which would have changed the number on the dashboard for a gate-only problem; and a persisted weekly plan table, which the last-week baseline makes unnecessary. Not done: a deterministic allocator that funds muscles in priority order. A follow-up the same day made that allocator unnecessary: capacity is now the median over completed, non-deload weeks (the rolling window had swept in the week in progress, a clipped edge week and both deloads, reading 39 against a true 54), and the direct-work floor became its own `direct_owed` number instead of a raised target, which was the same wrong-week defect in a second place. Demand then reads 49 against 54 and the week fits. The emphasis MEV-to-MAV midpoint floor is unchanged. The week after a deload now re-seeds at MEV by construction, since its baseline is the deload week. Enforced by `test_volume_target_is_built_on_last_week_not_the_week_in_progress`, `test_a_hold_holds_at_last_weeks_volume_not_at_zero`, `test_baseline_day_is_yellow_by_arithmetic_and_does_not_cap`, `test_block_starts_at_mev_not_at_the_curated_dose_range`, `test_observed_split_follows_the_days_actually_trained`, `test_capacity_is_measured_over_completed_non_deload_weeks`, `test_direct_work_shortfall_is_owed_not_added_to_the_target`.
